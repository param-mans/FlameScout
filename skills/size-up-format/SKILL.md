# Size-up format

A placeholder house style, to be replaced by the department's own card format.

## Order

Rank considerations in this order and drop from the bottom when you have more than five:

1. **Life safety**: reported or trapped occupants, where they were reported, the route to them.
2. **Fire location**: floor and side, including any disagreement between callers.
3. **Access**: entries, stairs, key box, the side the crew can actually reach.
4. **Water supply**: FDC, standpipes, hydrant-side approach if the data has it.
5. **Hazards**: tenant or occupancy hazards.
6. **Exposures and roof**: adjacent structures, roof access, ventilation.

## Titles

- Start with "Consider " and name one action and one place: "Consider Stair B from the rear alley".
- 14 words or fewer. No "and" joining two unrelated actions; split them or keep the more important.
- Advisory verbs only: consider, verify, be prepared for. Never: must, send, attack, go.

## Rationale

- One sentence, 25 words or fewer: the fact that makes the consideration worth reading.
- Say where the fact comes from when that changes how far to trust it:
  - caller reports: "a caller reported ..."
  - callers disagree: "callers disagree on ... (3 vs 4), unresolved"
  - aerial-only reads with no interior scan: "seen from the air, not verified"
- No numbers that are not in the files. No interior detail when there is no interior scan.

## When there is no interior scan

Say so once, in the most relevant consideration, and prefer considerations a crew can check from
outside (approach, visible entries, exposures, tenant hazards).
